Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Core Commitment: Ice Dodo Games is dedicated to offering a safe, free, and fun web gaming experience. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifying information (PII) from students or minors, and strictly respect your right to privacy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many online platforms, Ice Dodo Games operates on a no-registration, friction-free model:

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, home address, or phone number to play games.
  • We do not collect credit card numbers, payment details, or billing information.
  • We do not create individual user tracking profiles linked to personal identities.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit Ice Dodo Games (https://icedodogame.github.io/), our servers or third-party infrastructure providers (such as GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may automatically log standard technical data transmitted by your browser:

  • Technical Device Data: Browser type, operating system (e.g. ChromeOS on Chromebook, Windows, macOS), language preferences, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Information: Pages viewed, referring URLs, time spent playing, and game catalog interactions. This aggregate data helps us optimize game loading speeds and catalog selections.

3. LocalStorage & Cookies

We use modern browser technologies to enhance your experience:

  • HTML5 LocalStorage: Used exclusively on your local device to store your favorite games list (e.g., icedodo_favs) and game high scores. This data never leaves your computer or browser.
  • Session Cookies: May be used by third-party advertising partners or CDN networks to manage server load, ensure security, and detect bot traffic.

4. COPPA Compliance (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)

Ice Dodo Games is widely enjoyed by students in educational environments on school Chromebooks. We prioritize child safety and fully adhere to COPPA guidelines:

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.
  • If a parent, guardian, or educator believes that a child has inadvertently submitted personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.

5. Third-Party Games and External Links

Our website embeds games from third-party developers and hosts. While we vet all games to ensure they are safe, non-malicious, and appropriate for general audiences, these third-party game engines or external partners may have their own privacy policies. We encourage players to review third-party policies when interacting with external game content.

6. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To keep all games 100% free with zero paywalls, we may partner with reputable third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense). These partners may use anonymized cookies or web beacons to serve contextually relevant advertisements. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights to data access, rectification, and deletion. Because we do not collect personal identities or sell personal data, your privacy is safeguarded by default. If you have questions regarding your data rights, please contact our privacy coordinator.
